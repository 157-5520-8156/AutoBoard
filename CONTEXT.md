# Domain Glossary

## Economic Matter（经济事项）

A uniquely numbered procurement, investment, expense, engineering, or other
business matter that connects its budget, commitments, fulfilment, invoices,
payments, risks, and evidence. It is the financial-control module's unit of
review; it is not an accounting voucher or ledger account.

## Approved Budget（有效预算）

The sum of initial budget and approved increases, less approved reductions or
cancellations. Draft, rejected, withdrawn, or undocumented changes do not alter
the approved budget.

## Commitment（承诺）

An effective contract, order, investment agreement, approved non-contract
commitment, or other obligation ceiling currently undertaken for an economic
matter. Commitment is distinct from payment and from a recognised obligation.

## Recognised Obligation（已确认义务）

An amount supported by an accepted delivery, service confirmation, acceptance,
warehousing, or another documented event that establishes an obligation for
management-control purposes. Its accounting or tax treatment remains subject to
the authoritative finance system and qualified review.

## Paid Amount（已付款金额）

Completed cash payments allocated to an economic matter, excluding pending,
rejected, or reversed payments.

## Payable Amount（应付款金额）

Recognised obligation less paid amount, floored at zero. Due payable is the
portion whose due date has passed under the module's oldest-due-first
approximation; payment allocation detail must still be reviewed.

## Financial Alert（财务预警）

A deterministic rule result that identifies a condition requiring routine,
focused, or stop-and-check review. An alert is not a finding of fraud, a legal
opinion, an accounting conclusion, or permission to sign.

## Signature Advice（签前建议）

The module's review posture: routine review, focused review, or pause signature
and investigate. The authorised human remains responsible for the decision and
must review source evidence.
